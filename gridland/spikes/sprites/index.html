SPRITE SPIKE

Every pixel here is drawn by code (docs/ART.md). Characters come from inherited genes (skin, hair, eyes, hairstyle, build), plus age, job and mood. Each pair of panels shows the same thing at 16 px and 32 px per tile, scaled to the same on-screen size (64 px per tile), so you're comparing detail, not size.

THREE GENERATIONS

Grandparent → parent (with a partner) → their three children, shown twice: as children, then grown up. Each child takes each gene from one parent; skin can land between the two.

TWELVE JOBS

Clothing colour by job, plus a hat or a held tool for most jobs. Faces and hair are random here.

MOODS AND WALKING

Happy, neutral, sad, then the two walk frames, for one adult, one child and one elder.

A CORNER OF THE VILLAGE

Shoreline foam, path edges blending into grass, trees and roofs overlapping the tile above, drawn in depth order. Night uses a light map multiplied over the scene, the approach the new renderer would take. The small images are the true 1× size.
