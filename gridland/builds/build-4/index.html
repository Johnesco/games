      What to watch for

      • Two bots standing still together → chatting. Watch the bubbles.
      • Friends gift food memories when one is hungry.
      • Saplings → forests → full trees over hundreds of ticks.
      • Mature trees block movement — bots log a complaint; a toolmaker knaps a stone axe and fells the tree, which drops a log.
      • A digger hauls the log to a fire (fuel) or a stone home-ward (shrine).
      • A cook standing between a fire and a berry turns it into a cooked berry.
      • Rain leaves puddles — free drinks until they dry up.
      • Repeated walking over grass wears a path; paths thin out if unused.
      • Fires burn out into ash; ash decays back to grass or a sapling.
      • A fallen bot leaves a grave; healers mourn there and a flower sometimes blooms.
      • Warmth drops at night and in rain — bots drift toward fires and homes.
